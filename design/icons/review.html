ICONS · BATCH 1

The 12 effects and the 6 class ultimates. Pixel art like the drakes: one palette and pixel map per icon, drawn once, shown only at whole-number scales. Shape tells the category (buff: round badge, debuff: spiky badge, ultimate: a larger burst), colour tells the element. Each icon is shown next to the emoji it replaces, at the size it has in the game.
