ICONS · V2 ANIMATED · BATCHES 1–4 · THE WHITE DRAKE

The V2 set (section 0, design/icons/v2/): every icon redrawn at four times its glyph's density (a 10×10 glyph is 40×40 art, 12×12 is 48×48, the 11×11 roulette 44×44), each with an idle loop (an APNG that loops forever) and a still (its first frame, shown under prefers-reduced-motion and in screenshots). Every sheet below draws V2, as the overlay does: at the glyph's size × a whole number (×1, ×2, ×3), pixelated only where the size × devicePixelRatio is a whole multiple of the art (a 4K canvas: ×2 is 1:1), smoothed otherwise (1080p). The glyphs (icons.js) stay the set's record: their sizes, names, element colours and chips. Batch 1 (sections 1–5): the 12 effects and the 6 class ultimates. Batch 2 (sections 6–8): the log notes, the title mechanics and the attack types. Batch 3 (sections 9–10): Jackpot's reels, the title bonuses and frames, the medals and the UI. The White Drake (sections 14–15): its scale bar, Перекування, Магніт, Удар ковадла, «Біла сталь», its emblem, and the streamer card. The emblems (section 16): the elements' symbols on a tile. Batch 4 (sections 11–13): attacking, the title roulette, the meter's «ready», the title-roll card, the arena plate and the Twitch reward icons. No frames on the icons: an effect's or a note's glyph has the whole 10×10, an ultimate its own 12×12 silhouette. Buff or debuff is the chip's shape (rounded or angled ends), colour is the element. A title badge is its rarity frame's loop with its mechanic's note glyph on top, one glyph pixel in. Each icon is shown next to the emoji it replaces, at the size it has in the game.

16 · THE EMBLEMS: THE ELEMENTS' SYMBOLS

IN PLACE · ×1
IN PLACE · ×2
Each drake's emblem is its element's symbol on a tile (top row: the still and the loop at 1:1, ×4). The tile is the same for every drake, only its colours change: a frame in the element's colour, its light edge up and left and its dark edge down and right, a dark fill, and a round disc a shade lighter behind the symbol. The symbol tells them apart by shape, not only by colour: Red a molten rock split by glowing cracks (fire), Blue a cluster of ice crystals, Black a violet crescent moon, Gold a four-leaf clover, Purple an arcane orb in two crossing orbits, Green a sprout; the White Dragon its green eye, the White Drake a green flame in a steel ring. node design/icons/test.js checks it: the eight are one tile, and every two symbols (the pixels inside the tile that aren't the tile's, colour ignored) differ in at least 64 art pixels, 4 glyph pixels at the 4× density. Lit from the top left like every icon, an emblem is never mirrored: on the title plate all six face the same way. In place: the queue (instead of the old 14 px colour square), the Top (before the level; the sprite stays), the card's name line, the log banners and the arena's title plate.
