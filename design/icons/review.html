Batch 1 (sections 1–5): the 12 effects and the 6 class ultimates. Batch 2 (sections 6–8): the log notes, the title mechanics and the attack types. Pixel art like the drakes: one palette and pixel map per icon, drawn once, shown only at whole-number scales. No frames: an effect's or a note's glyph has the whole 10×10, an ultimate its own 12×12 silhouette. Buff or debuff is the chip's shape (rounded or angled ends), colour is the element. A title badge is its mechanic's note glyph in a 12×12 rarity frame. Each icon is shown next to the emoji it replaces, at the size it has in the game.
